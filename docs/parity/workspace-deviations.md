| Row | Deviation | Reason |

| WS-06, OVV-003, SPL-009 | [AI] A reload that fails (a file gone, a decoder or option refused) leaves the viewer playing its old Content and playhead, with a dismissible `Could not reload: <reason>` that returns for the next failure, and not when the files changed again meanwhile (the offer stands). The reference said `entry N` and opened the media in the same step | Items resolve as a whole; reload media is validated before commit, cumulatively covering every entry a surviving viewer will show after the commit while the same resolved Content stays in the job. | WS-15, EXP-014, EXP-017 | [human] "Keep the rewrite's retry-after-failure, ETag and disk cache; add a worker cap, an in-memory LRU and a camera cooldown." [AI] Both caches exist in the reference: it keeps first-frame JPEGs on disk (500, fingerprint-keyed, written atomically, newest kept), so the owner's answer rested on a premise that was wrong for the disk cache; the rewrite keeps the same 500. [AI] What differs: a failed picture is asked for again on the next refresh, where the reference stopped tracking it; a file's picture carries an ETag, which the reference, with no HTTP between picture and card, had no use for; the in-memory pictures (48, least recently used first out) are keyed by the file's identity, so they need no freshness interval, where the reference reused a picture for 10 s from the start of its read; a camera, login and head are asked at most once per 10 s from when the last read finished (the reference counted from the start), for as long as the key is among the 48 kept, so once 48 other keys have been asked for it can be asked again within 10 s; reads are capped at 2 for files, as the reference's two workers were for files and cameras | Owner decision on retry, ETag and the caps; the rest follows from the rewrite's design |
